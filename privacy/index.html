English

Recall Deck does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account, no tracking, no ads. The app has no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The only part of it that goes online is the app store's purchase service (see Purchases).
  • Your cards stay on your device. Decks, cards, images and settings are saved only in the app's own storage on your device.
  • System backups. If you turn on your device's own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud or Time Machine on Apple devices), the system may include the app's data in it. That backup is kept in your own account under that service's terms; we never have access to it.
  • Purchases. If you buy the full version, the payment is handled by Google Play (or the App Store on Apple devices) under its own terms. We never receive your payment details. The app only learns from the store whether the full version has been bought, and keeps that on your device. On Android, Google's purchase library inside the app may also send Google technical diagnostics about the purchase service (for example whether it could connect), under Google's privacy policy.
  • Files you choose. The app reads a file only when you pick or drop one yourself (for example an image, a list to import, or a backup), and writes a file only when you export a deck or create a backup. Backups go where you save them.
  • Pronunciation. For language decks, words are read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech service. Recall Deck does not send your cards anywhere; how the system voice service works is governed by your device maker's privacy policy.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting a deck in the app removes its cards and images. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

中文

Recall Deck 不收集、不存储、也不分享任何个人数据。

  • 无需账号，没有追踪，没有广告。 应用没有登录、没有数据统计、没有广告。唯一会联网的是应用商店的购买服务（见“购买”）。
  • 卡片只保存在你的设备上。 卡组、卡片、图片和设置只保存在本应用在你设备上的存储空间里。
  • 系统备份。 如果你开启了设备自带的备份（例如安卓备份到你的 Google 账号，或苹果设备的 iCloud、时间机器），系统可能会把本应用的数据一并备份。这份备份保存在你自己的账号里，受该服务的条款约束，我们无法访问。
  • 购买。 如果你购买完整版，付款由 Google Play（苹果设备上是 App Store）按其条款处理。我们不会收到你的付款信息；应用只从商店得知是否已购买完整版，并把这个结果保存在你的设备上。在安卓上，应用内置的 Google 购买组件还可能向 Google 发送购买服务的技术诊断信息（例如能否连接），受 Google 隐私政策约束。
  • 只访问你选择的文件。 只有当你亲自选择或拖入文件时（例如图片、要导入的列表或备份文件），应用才会读取它；只有当你导出卡组或创建备份时，应用才会写出文件，保存位置由你决定。
  • 发音。 语言学习卡组的朗读使用设备自带的文字转语音服务。Recall Deck 不会把你的卡片发送到任何地方；系统语音服务如何工作由设备厂商的隐私政策说明。
  • 删除数据。 在应用里删除卡组会同时删除其中的卡片和图片；卸载应用会删除它保存的全部内容。
